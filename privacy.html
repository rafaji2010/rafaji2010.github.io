Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

This application is a personal tool used only by its developer, to sync the developer's own notes to the developer's own Google Drive account.

What it accesses

It requests the drive.file scope, which lets it see and manage only the files and folders that it creates itself. It cannot read any other file in the Google Drive account.

What it collects and shares

It collects no data from any other person. The developer's data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Files are copied only between the developer's computer and the developer's Google Drive.

Deleting access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
